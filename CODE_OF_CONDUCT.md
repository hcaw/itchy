# Code of conduct

Itchy is a toy for children, built by people who care about how children learn and play. We want everyone who takes
part, whatever their age, background, identity or experience, to feel welcome and safe.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- **Be kind.** Be patient and welcoming, especially with people who are new to code, to music or to open source.
- **Be constructive.** Critique ideas, not people. Assume good intent, and accept feedback gracefully.
- **Be respectful.** No harassment, insults, sexualised language or imagery, trolling or personal attacks, in public or
  in private.
- **Respect privacy.** Never publish anyone's private information without their permission.

## Keeping children safe

Because Itchy is used by children, there is one extra rule. **Never share personal information about a child** in
issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project. That includes names, photos, videos, voice
recordings, school names and anything else that could identify them. If you need to show how a child used Itchy,
describe it in words. Maintainers will remove anything that breaks this rule straight away.

## Reporting

If you experience or see behaviour that breaks this code, contact the maintainer, [@hcaw](https://github.com/hcaw),
privately. Every report will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy will be
respected.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they judge to be harmful, following the
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines) of
the Contributor Covenant.
